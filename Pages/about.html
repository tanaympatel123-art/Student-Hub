About StudentHub

What is StudentHub?

StudentHub is a unified web portal developed to provide students, faculty, and campus staff with a centralized platform for academic records, course tracking, and campus updates.

Core Objectives

  • Provide seamless access to academic and student services.
  • Centralize departmental and campus-wide announcements.
  • Facilitate real-time event discovery and registration.
  • Maintain secure, role-based student and faculty profiles.
  • Streamline administrative workflows and student feedback loops.

Key Modules

  1. Authentication & RBAC: Dedicated portals for Students, Faculty, and Administrators.
  2. Academic Dashboard: Course syllabus, attendance metrics, and grade tracking.
  3. Campus Life: Filterable event schedules and real-time notice feeds.
  4. Support & Feedback: Direct ticketing and suggestion channels.
